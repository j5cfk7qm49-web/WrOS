WrOS

WrOS turns a laptop into a writing computer: it starts straight into a distraction-free writing app, keeps books safe with verified backups, and updates itself with signed updates.

WrOS can connect the writer's own Google Drive, so a book can be backed up there and edited from other devices. Connecting is done from the writer's phone: the laptop shows a QR code, the phone signs in to Google, and the approval goes straight back to the laptop.
