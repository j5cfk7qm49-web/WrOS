WrOS privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026.

What WrOS collects

WrOS is software that runs on the writer's own laptop. The WrOS project runs no server that receives your data, your files or your Google account details, and collects no analytics.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, WrOS asks Google for access to your Drive so it can back up your books there and keep them in step with files you add or change from other devices.

  • Google's approval is a one-use code. It passes through this site's address in your phone's browser, then goes to your laptop over your local network. It is useless without a secret that never leaves your laptop. The access it grants is stored only on your laptop.
  • Your files travel only between your laptop and Google Drive.
  • WrOS uses this access only to back up, restore and sync the books you set up, at your request. It does not sell, share or transfer your data, and does not use it for advertising.
  • WrOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This website

This site is static and hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies. GitHub may record visits as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The linking page reads the QR code's link only inside your browser.
