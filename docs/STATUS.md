## Measurement/performance boundaries

The [executed synthetic release-mode probe](evidence/accounting-benchmark.json) measured 30 samples per source size: 10 kB median **640 µs**, 50 kB **2,974 µs**, 200 kB **13,129 µs**, 260 kB **19,583 µs**. A 300 kB source deliberately produced 30 explicit word gaps. Thirty actual Editor insertions on a 50 kB fixture took **95,120 µs**; 1,024 synthetic ledger groups flushed in **16,680 µs** and loaded in **3,651 µs**. These are shared-sandbox microbenchmarks, **not full UI latency, physical low-power/battery/flash endurance or loaded-analyzer certification**. Prefix publication avoids a full part rewrite/fsync per individual group.

Checkpoints are intended at 30 seconds plus explicit save/normal quit. **An uncheckpointed crash tail can be lost; recorded totals are not complete all-day coverage.** Per-file 1 MiB / 128-group parts, 4,096 pending groups and bounded 16,384-file / 64 MiB reload are initial limits, not indefinite archival. Optional awards cap at 512; no new badge at capacity, no source/metric penalty. Runtime/ledger failure and optional badge-only failure remain distinct.

Guarded fingerprints are not OS locks, hostile-race CAS, authenticated cross-process measurement, cross-file atomicity, power-cut proof or backup. Controlled partial-failure/retry tests do not prove abrupt kernel/power behavior. Unknown/corrupt/future/conflicting metadata and originals are retained.
