# Daily challenges, goals and productivity accounting

**Advanced daily challenges/achievements/accounting: Planned (M5).** Existing Grimoire daily/book/scene targets, net-growth records, recent pace, Pomodoro and basic sprints are reused and shown in the current dashboard. They are not gross-typing or active-time metrics.

## Assignment model

Use the **local civil calendar date** and recorded IANA timezone, not UTC midnight or elapsed 24-hour windows. Persist each day's assigned record IDs, corpus version, difficulty/duration/profile filters, accepted/rerolled/completed state and whether completion is measured or self-reported. Reboot does not reroll. A timezone change does not destroy another day's assignment; a repeated date retrieves its stored assignment. DST 23/25-hour days remain one civil date.

Categories: productivity, creative writing, character, plot, dialogue, description, point of view, revision, read-through and Story Spark combinations. Offer a small optional selection, explicit rerolls and recent-repeat avoidance. Filters can be drafting-only, revision-only or mixed, short/long and adjustable difficulty. Disabling challenges also disables their notifications; focus mode never forces them onto the page.

## Accurate metrics first

Use a monotonic active-session clock with configurable idle cutoff. Record edit operations to distinguish gross words added, words removed and net manuscript change; don't derive gross from positive final counts. Separate drafting/revision, manuscript/notes/research and optional footnote accounting. UTC instants plus local-date grouping make time labels auditable. Automatic journal facts remain distinct from reflections and self-reported creative achievements.

Goals: daily/weekly/monthly/novel/scene/time/sprint/revision and configurable deadlines. The offline 50,000-word November preset needs start/end dates, optional rest days, required remaining-day pace, calendar, ahead/behind and a forecast that clearly states its assumptions. No external account or endorsement.

## Sprints and achievements

Extend current sprint plumbing with time, word, revision, no-editing and challenge-linked variants, pause/resume, optional breaks and results/records. Do not silently discard a deletion in a no-editing experiment; a restriction must be explicit and reversible.

Achievements are disableable, encouraging and optional. Milestones, finished scenes/chapters, revisions, experimentation and learning can count without punitive uninterrupted streaks. Quiet notifications and a collection view should respect focus settings.

## Acceptance and tutorial

Deterministic clocks/seeds/date providers; DST/timezone travel; reboot/no unnecessary regeneration; rerolls/history exhaustion; difficulty and duration; measured versus self-report; idle gaps, revisions/deletions/undo/paste; footnote counts; disabling everything; sprint pause/resume; non-punitive achievement dedup. Tutorial lessons 17 and 19 currently exercise baseline goals/sprints; lessons 18, 20 and 21 remain planned for the advanced subsystem.
