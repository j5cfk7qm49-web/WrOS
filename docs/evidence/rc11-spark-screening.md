# Story Spark editorial screening for rc11

Codex performed this screening on 2026-11-10. The corpus digest was
`47563aa3bd16251b9a08ca30d12f78901530f1a87c0d0b5c3137f8dd6e153ec4`.
It contains 1,047 accepted plots and 84 exercises. Independent human editorial
and rights review remains open in
[issue 12](https://github.com/j5cfk7qm49-web/WrOS/issues/12).

The screening covered the midpoint plot from each of the fourteen source
files, the first exercise in each of the fourteen exercise categories, and
all 32 closest lexical pairs in the existing report. Each text was checked
for a concrete task or conflict, an intelligible constraint, and a reason
for the writer to make a choice. Each pair was compared by the mechanism
that creates its obstacle and the decision it asks the protagonist to make.

## Pair review

The strongest follow-up candidate is **wd-p-0673 and wd-p-0935**. Both make
vehicle drinking water compete with completing the mission. One uses it to
repair a rescue engine, while the other uses it to cool ecological samples.
A human reviewer should decide whether the change in mission is enough
to justify retaining both. The existing hold on wd-p-0986 already covers a
closely related cooling-samples dilemma.

**wd-p-0702 and wd-p-0997** share renovation as a way to expose incompatible
expectations in a relationship. Their decisions differ. One concerns whether
continuing a shared project postpones a separation, and the other concerns
whether a promised shared space has become private. They merit a joint
editorial read because both use the same broad device.

The other reviewed pairs have distinguishable obstacles. Examples include
wd-p-0279's heat-signature problem and wd-p-0628's timed radio contact, and
wd-p-0779's identity correction and wd-p-0799's accounting of unpaid work.
Two pairs in the report involve existing holdouts wd-p-0679 and wd-p-0986.
Those texts remain in the draft sources for review.
