# Getting started

WrOS is a writing desk for one project at a time. The project is a folder of plain Markdown files, so everything you write can be read, copied and backed up without WrOS. You can have as many projects as you like: *File › New project…* starts another, and *File › Open another project…* switches (see *Your projects*).

Projects you start by name go in `~/Documents/Grimoire`. *Project › This project › Where this project is kept…* shows the current folder. From there you can open it or save a ZIP copy.

## The desk

- **The outline**, on the left, headed by the project's title: parts, chapters and scenes, then the notebook (Characters, Places, Notes, Research) and the rest of the project's sections.
- **The page**, on the right: the scene you're writing.
- **The Pomodoro**, under the outline: a timer drawn as a little world. `←` `→` turns it into the Visualizer.
- **Music**, below that, when you've turned it on.
- **The top bar**, with W r O S tiles for Write, read, Outline and Study, the menus, save and backup marks, and the ⏻ Power tile.
- **The upper bottom bar**, with information about where you are on the left and Wi-Fi, sound, battery and the clock on the right. Click a widget to open its panel.
- **The lower bottom bar**, with the project's words against its target and today's words on the left, and messages on the right. When there is no fresh message, the playing track scrolls there.

## Getting around

- `Tab` and `Shift-Tab` move between the panes.
- `Esc` lights **the menu bar** along the top, from anywhere; a menu's underlined letter (or `↓`) opens it. The highlighted row says what it does on the lower bottom bar, and `?` opens the help on it (see *The menu bar, menu by menu*). `Esc` again closes it and leaves you where you were. `F10` opens the bar too, and so does a click on a menu's name. From File, `←` reaches the view tiles. Click a tile or use `Super+1` through `Super+4` to switch views.
- `Ctrl-K`, or a tap of `Super`, **finds anything**: every action, scene, note and theme by a few letters of its name, with the key that does the same thing and the menu it lives in.
- `?` or `F1` opens this help, on the topic for wherever you are. On the page, and in any box you type into, `?` is just a question mark, so use `F1` there.

On a Mac, where the terminal can send it, `Cmd` works everywhere `Ctrl` does.

## Saving and quitting

You never have to save. A scene is written two seconds after you stop typing, and never waits longer than twenty seconds. `Ctrl-S` saves at once if you like.

Installed WrOS hides Quit and shows a short message when you press `Ctrl-Q`, keeping the writing app open. To stop writing, use *Power › Lock*, or *Power › Shut down…*. *Settings › System › Battery and power* also offers Restart the writing app, which keeps you signed in. In a desktop session, use `Ctrl-Q`, `q` in the outline, or *Power › Quit*. These save and close the app.

## The mouse

On WrOS's own screen the pointer glides with your hand, a pixel at a time, without waiting for the page to redraw.

Click a pane to move to it, a chapter to fold it, a scene to open it, and the prose to put the cursor there. Drag across the prose to select; double-click picks a word, and a third click the paragraph. The wheel scrolls whatever is under the pointer.

Click a row in the menu to choose it. Right-click a row in the outline to open the *Project* menu for it: rename, delete, details, a new scene.

From a terminal, `grimoire wros help` lists the WrOS appliance commands.

## About WrOS

Open *Help › About WrOS*. You can also find About WrOS in `Ctrl-K`. It shows what WrOS is built on and who made it: WrOS is built on GrimoireTUI, by Josh King and Catfinity Studios. If a browser is available, `Enter`, or a click on the link, opens catfinity.com. Otherwise WrOS shows the address to open on another device. `l` opens the License topic. Catfinity Studios also makes **Catfinity**, an idle cat RPG for iPhone and Android: `c` in About WrOS (or a click on its line) attempts to open its page and shows its address if a browser cannot open. From a terminal, `grimoire --version` says the same.

## Donate

GrimoireTUI, which WrOS is built on, is free, and a donation is never required. It's always appreciated, though, and it all goes toward GrimoireTUI's development. Open *Help › Donate…*. This opens the Donate dialog, as do Donate (Ko-fi) in `Ctrl-K` and `d` in About WrOS. If a browser is available, `Enter` opens Ko-fi. Otherwise WrOS shows the address to open on another device.
