# The menu bar, menu by menu

The top bar starts with **W r O S**, the tiles for Write, read, Outline and Study. Then come **File, Edit, View, Project, Tools, Settings, Help** and **Power** at the right. On the native graphical screen, the view letters and ⏻ appear in square tiles. In the text layout, look for plain W r O S and Power.

`Esc` lights the bar on the menu you used last; `←` `→` move along it, and `↓` or `Enter` opens the lit one. `←` from File reaches the view tiles; choose one and press `Enter` to switch. A menu's underlined letter opens it straight away, so *Esc, then P* opens Project and *Esc, then W* opens Power. In the graphical session, press `Alt-P` for Project or `Alt` with another menu's letter. `F10` opens the bar too. A tap of the `Super` key opens **Find anything** just under the bar; `←` or `→` from there goes into the menus.

Menus drop down in the header's colour with a shadow. Inside a menu, `↑` `↓` (or `j` `k`) move, a letter jumps to the next row starting with it, and `Enter` runs the row. Rows with a › open beside it. The highlighted row says what it does on the lower bottom bar, and `?` opens the help on it. A row that can't run right now is dim and says why. `Esc` closes the menu.

The mouse works everywhere on the bar: click a view tile to switch views, click a menu to open it, move across the titles to switch, click a row to run it, use the wheel to move through a menu, and click anywhere else to close it.

## Power

- **Install WrOS to disk…**: only on a live USB stick.
- **Lock** saves and locks right away. Unlock to get back to where you were writing.
- **Sleep**, **Restart…** and **Shut down…** ask first, as in Settings › System › Battery and power.
- **Quit** (`Ctrl-Q`): where WrOS offers it; everything is saved first.

## File

- **Settle conflicts…**: only there when a sync service has left two copies of a scene that disagree. See *Sync & conflicts*.
- **New project…** and **Open another project…**: see *Your projects*. On the shelf, *Import a project…* brings in a project from Google Drive, a USB stick or this laptop. Choose *A draft (Word, Markdown, text)* there for a draft file, or *Open a copy…* on the shelf for a project ZIP.
- **MLA 9 paper details…**: set up the details for an MLA paper before export.
- **Export…**: the manuscript as a Word file, a PDF, an EPUB or a paperback. See *Compile*.
- **Compile to Markdown** and **Update the project map**: see *On disk*.
- **Back up now**: the open project, to the places Settings › Backups and accounts names.
- **Writing sessions…** and **Save this session now**: your sittings, like a diary. See *Writing sessions*.
- **Move writing history out…**: only for an older project in a synced folder. See *Writing sessions*.

## Edit

- **Undo** (`Ctrl-Z`), **Redo** (`Ctrl-Y`), **Cut** (`Ctrl-X`), **Copy** (`Ctrl-C`), **Paste** (`Ctrl-V`) and **Select all** (`Ctrl-A`): in the page you're writing.
- **Find and replace…** (`Ctrl-F`): in this scene; press it again for the whole project.
- **Search the whole project…** (`/`): a word or phrase in every scene and note, with replace. See *Find & replace*.
- **Italic** (`Ctrl-I`) and **Bold** (`Ctrl-B`): on the selection or the word at the cursor, and off again.
- **Insert footnote here**.

## View

- The W r O S tiles switch views: **Write** (`Super+1`) returns to the manuscript, **read** (`Super+2`) shows works and editions, **Outline** (`Super+3`) opens your personal outline, and **Study** (`Super+4`) opens cards. You can also use **Find anything**.
- In read, press `F` to edit the active filter. An empty filter shows all works. Use `Page Up` and `Page Down` when the list spans several pages.
- **Study** opens cards made in Outline notes. Press Enter to reveal an answer, then choose a grade from 0 to 5. Press F for a tag or saved search. The S tile turns red when cards are due; the upper bottom bar shows how many are left today.
- Outline opens to **Today**. Press `N` to add a note, `Enter` to edit or save, and `Esc` to cancel an edit. Use `↑` and `↓` to choose a note, `→` to open its children, and `←` to go back. `P` shows named pages, where `N` adds a page. `T` returns to Today.
- **Outline beside Write** opens the notes tagged for this project. A note created in that list receives the project tag. Press `Esc` outside an edit to return to the manuscript, or select the W tile. You can also click either pane.
- Press `I` for a note's details, including its tags, fields and links. `@` adds a link by title, `O` opens linked notes, and `B` opens backlinks. Press `#` to tag a note. Use a built-in name like `book` or the title of a named page.
- Press `/` to search, `S` to save a search on the selected note, and `R` to run that saved search. Use a phrase, `#tag`, `@title` for backlinks, or `field=value` when the selected note has that field.
- In **Settings › Backups and accounts › Backups**, create an encrypted Commonplace backup and restore earlier Commonplace snapshots to a separate folder.
- **Focus mode** (`Ctrl-D`): the page fills the screen. Urgent warnings appear on the bottom line when needed. `Ctrl-D` again brings back the bars and panes.
- **Show the outline** / **Hide the outline**: the outline column, without entering focus mode. It has no key of its own unless you give it one in Settings › Look and keyboard › Keyboard.
- **Show the pins sidebar** / **Hide the pins sidebar**. See *Pins*.
- **Hide Outline beside Write** closes the project notes pane.
- **Open a scene beside…** (`v`).
- **Show the second pane** / **Hide the second pane** (`F11`) and **Choose second document…**: a second document you can edit beside the first.
- **Themes…** (`F9`) and **Workspaces…**.

## Project

- **New scene…** (`n`), **New chapter…** (`c`), **New part…** (`p`) and **New folder…** (`N`). If your project calls its parts acts, the row says so.
- **Rename…** (`r`) and **Delete…** (`d`): the file is renamed to match; deleting moves it to the Trash and asks first.
- **Empty the Trash…** replaces Delete when the Trash itself is selected. It permanently removes the items counted when the question appeared.
- **Restore from the Trash** (`u`): with something in the Trash selected.
- **Move up** (`K`) and **Move down** (`J`): you can also drag rows with the mouse.
- Switch to Write to move, rename, restore or delete a selected project item.
- **Scene details…**: status, POV, synopsis, word target, and whether it goes in exports. See *The outline*.
- **Scene history…** (`H`): the scene's versions from the timeline and saved copies. See *History & recovery*.
- **This project ›** opens these rows:
  - **Where this project is kept…** shows the folder, sync and backup status, and lets you open the folder or move the project into `~/Documents`.
  - **Save a copy (one file)** saves a ZIP with the project name and date in `~/Documents/Copies`.
  - **Goals…** sets the word targets for the project and for each day.

## Tools

- **Start a sprint…** / **Stop the sprint**: see *Sprints & targets*.
- **Start the timer** / **Pause the timer** (`F2`) and **Reset the timer** (`F3`): the Pomodoro.
- **Read aloud**, **Read chapter aloud** and **Read manuscript aloud**. See *Revision*.
- **Music player…** (`F7`): while music is on.
- **Dictionary and thesaurus…** and **Writing checks…**.
- **Revision ›**: Notes and TKs (`Ctrl-T`), Next TK or note, Next scene still in draft, Turn echo words on or off, Check names, Corkboard, Footnote navigator, Read footnote at caret, Continuity tracker, Draft milestones, Revision notes and Prose and revision dashboard.
- **Notes ›**: read (`Super+2`), Look up the name here (`Ctrl-O`), Pinboard, Pin this document, Writing journal, Scratchpad, Session handoff, Project sources, Add a source, Import sources, Project sources timeline and Resolve source conflict.
- **Progress ›** holds Progress (the project's word count and pace), Goals, Calendar, Sprints, Edits, Collection, Challenges and Story Spark.

## Settings

**Writing**, **Look and keyboard**, **System**, **Backups and accounts**, **Updates and recovery** and **Music**: each opens Settings on that group, in a box over the page. See *Settings*.

Under **Updates and recovery › Updates**, choose **Firmware for a new device** to look for matching firmware for a device you've plugged in. When offline, plug in a WrOS USB stick before you choose the row. See *WrOS*.

## Help

- **Help…** (`?`, or `F1` from anywhere): this.
- **Interactive tutorial…**: lessons you do, not read.
- **License…**: free to use, even for work you sell. Credit is all it asks.
- **Donate…**: GrimoireTUI, which WrOS is built on, is free, and a tip on Ko-fi is always appreciated, never required.
- **About WrOS**: what it's built on and who made it, and the license.
